## Independent attack and admission

`tests/tools/symbolic.py` is an independent attacker/checker, outside the generic
host. It constructs wrapping-byte polynomial formulas for ordered input/output
events, summarizes pure triangular loops and unrolls known finite loops. It does
not emit protected source or use private CUM templates or recovery information.
Only fully summarized programs within explicit operation, term, degree and
verified safe-cell bounds produce a completed projection. Input-dependent I/O
loops, unproved termination, unsupported loops, invalid inputs and exhausted
analysis budgets block this check; they do not establish resistance.

Equal ordered I/O formulas prove recovery in this scoped model. Unequal polynomial
forms are inconclusive: polynomials over the wrapping-byte ring do not have a
unique representation as functions. This is a sufficient equality attack, not a
general equivalence decision procedure or a replacement for matched AI trials.
Projection status PASS means analysis completed, never that resistance passed.

The existing mandatory BF `normalization` check now runs this attack after
instruction/affine and actual BBC O1 BFX comparisons. Equal projections write a
FAIL record and stop prepare before signing. Missing operands or unsupported
analysis write BLOCKED. Starting a check invalidates an older PASS report.
Unequal formulas also block: this conservative checker presently has no BF
release-admission path. An independently reviewed stronger analysis and its
acceptance contract are required before an unsupported candidate can progress.
The actual prepare planner already requests this check; no extra host-side CUM
selection or language transformation is introduced. The fixture VM test expects
the known weak transfer fixture to fail normalization while preserving its
independently checked behavior/build/boundary results.

Re-pin the entire independent check kit including `tests/tools/symbolic.py` and
supply `symbolic_analysis` in its binding. The example cell count is deliberately

Prior retained check kits and signed results are historical; their identities
